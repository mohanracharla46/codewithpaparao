Meet your instructor

PapaRao Rapuri

Founder & Lead Instructor, Code With PapaRao

Hello! I'm PapaRao Rapuri, an educator and programming mentor passionate about helping students build strong problem-solving skills through Programming, Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), and Computer Science fundamentals.

With 15+ years of teaching and technical training experience, I have guided thousands of students from beginner to advanced levels. My mission is to simplify complex concepts using real-world examples, visual explanations, and structured learning, enabling every student to solve coding problems with confidence.

I founded Code With PapaRao to provide high-quality, industry-oriented programming education that bridges the gap between academic learning and technical interviews. My teaching emphasizes algorithmic thinking, problem-solving, time complexity, memory optimization, and writing efficient code.

I believe anyone can become a great programmer with the right guidance and consistent practice. Through Code With PapaRao, my goal is to make programming simple, practical, and accessible while preparing students for placements, competitive exams, and successful software engineering careers.

My Teaching Philosophy

Practical First

Theory is important, but true understanding comes through writing code. All lessons include real-world assignments, lab projects, and interactive quizzes to reinforce concepts.

Under the Hood

We don't skip the hard parts. You'll learn how programs allocate memory, compile instructions, manage call stacks, and run algorithms under constraints.
